# Rule: Zero AI Slop in Design, Code, and Copy

## Scope
Applies to all UI templates, CSS stylesheets, copywriting, and documentation.

## Requirements
1. **Design Cleanliness**:
   - No gradient backgrounds (linear or radial).
   - No glow effects, bloom filters, or colored box shadows.
   - No glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur` with translucent highlights).
   - No animated floating blobs, sparkles, or particle canvasses.
   - No decorative stock imagery or generic hero graphics.
2. **Copywriting**:
   - Banned words: "seamless", "unleash", "supercharge", "elevate", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "magic", "effortless", "intelligent".
   - State facts, numerical specifications, verified prices, and direct verdicts only.
3. **Typography and Layout**:
   - Neutral monochrome color tokens only.
   - Inter or Geist font family.
   - Thin 1px borders with maximum 6px border radii.
